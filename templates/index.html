Use the My Almost Mommy Generator to discover which of your father’s former girlfriends is attending this year's Harvard-Yale football game!

The inspiration for AlmostGilmore.com came from the confluence of two iconic aspects of 2000's pop culture. First, the legendary The Wu-Tang Name Generator, the same tool that famously gave Donald Glover his stage name, Childish Gambino. Second, the classic (and perfectly cringy) dialogue from Gilmore Girls where Lorelai Gilmore refers to her father’s college sweetheart, Pennilyn Lott, as “my almost Mommy.”

This page was created to celebrate a niche piece of TV history while practicing full-stack programming. You can find the code for this project on Github. Making this site was a ton of fun for me, and I hope you have fun playing around as well!

Note: This is a fan-made parody site and is not affiliated with Gilmore Girls, Warner Bros., or the Wu-Tang Clan.
